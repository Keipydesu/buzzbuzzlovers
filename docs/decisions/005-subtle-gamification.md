# 005 — Keep gamification subtle

Date: 2026-09-26.

Status: accepted presentation direction; specific goals and milestones are provisional.

The personal-only emphasis is superseded by [decision 011](011-social-competition-direction.md), which makes friend-group competition a major part of the planned experience.

## Context

Following the focused tracker direction, the user selected goals, rings, and milestones as the preferred amount of gamification.

## Decision

Use a compact goal ring and understated milestone rewards while keeping measurements and history prominent. Exact thresholds and reward rules remain open.

## Consequences

The first Rails page is a clearly labeled sample-data preview with light and dark modes, as requested. Its illustrative 20-minute goal and milestones are not validated health targets or a finalized reward system. The preview uses no live wearable integration and writes no sample records.

The tracking-duration goal example is superseded by [decision 010](010-reward-slouch-reduction.md).
